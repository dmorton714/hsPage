How It Works (The Process)
From Registration to Retouched Headshot in Minutes
1. Attendees Reserve Their Spot
Guests can sign up before the event through a custom registration page with your custom advertising and select an available time slot.
2. They Visit the Headshot Studio
At their scheduled time, attendees step into our professional on-site studio where our photographers guide them through a fast, comfortable session. You have the ability to brand or have staff to talk about products and interact with attendees. 
3. They Choose Their Favorite
Instead of waiting days to see what they received, attendees can review their images and choose the headshot they want. This is done in real time with quick previews. Building an unmatched and comfortable experience. 
4. We Handle the Rest
Their selected image is automatically moved into our editing workflow, professionally retouched, processed, and prepared for delivery.
5. Their Headshot Arrives Within Minutes
In most cases, attendees receive their finished professional headshot within approximately five minutes of completing their session (internet speeds dictate timing).
No waiting several days
No wondering which image the photographer chose
No complicated delivery process
Just a professional headshot ready to use
No hidden Fees
